## Children

ReadTrail is a general productivity / lifestyle reading tracker. It does not target children under 13, does not create accounts, and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data. Do not enrol in Designed for Families / Kids categories unless product and privacy copy are rewritten for that audience.

## Account deletion

Not applicable for cloud accounts — none exist. Local wipe: Settings → Clear all data.
